For industries heavily reliant on seasonal workers, the scarcity of qualified talent has become a concern. Whether it's agriculture, hospitality, or retail, the demand for temporary employees surges during peak seasons, leaving employers grappling with the challenge of attracting and retaining top-tier seasonal workers. We do recommend some strategies for attracting the best talent along with new innovative approaches to secure and retain seasonal workers.
