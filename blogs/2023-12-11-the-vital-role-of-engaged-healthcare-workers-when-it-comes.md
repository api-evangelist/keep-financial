Healthcare institutions are the backbone of our society, responsible for the well-being of individuals in times of need. To ensure the highest standards of care and safety, hospitals and healthcare facilities must adhere to a multitude of regulations and compliance standards. A dip in service levels and compliance can lead to an array of issues - and many of these issues stem from a lack of engagement by workers.
